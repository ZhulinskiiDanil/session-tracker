# Changelog

## v1.1.0

- Import from Death Tracker (Levels tab): its sessions, deaths by percent and play time come in as sessions, its linked levels as groups. Its files are only read, importing again only brings in what's new, and sessions both mods recorded keep the ones here
- The merge list shows the 100 most recently played levels, search finds the rest

## v1.0.0

- Sessions per level: play time with pauses apart, attempts (normal, start position, practice), best percent, completions and deaths by percent
- Copies of a level, copies of copies and the original counted as one group; levels the game can't link merged and split by hand
- Stats popup with Session, Overview, Sessions and Levels tabs, from level pages and the pause menu
- Export of a group's sessions to CSV
