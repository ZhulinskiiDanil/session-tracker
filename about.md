# Session Tracker

Keeps a history of every time you play a level:

- **Sessions**: when you played, for how long (time on pause counted apart), how many attempts
- **Attempts**: normal, from a start position and in practice mode, each counted separately
- **Deaths**: your best percent and the percents you die at the most
- **Copies merged**: a copy of a level, a copy of that copy and the original all count as one level. Anything the game can't link on its own, you can merge (or split) by hand

Open the stats with the clock button on a level's page or in the pause menu:

- **Session**: the session you're playing right now
- **Overview**: totals, deaths by percent and time per session
- **Sessions**: every session, expandable for the details, and an export to CSV
- **Levels**: the levels counted together, to merge or split
